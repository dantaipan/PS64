PS64 1.0.2: a new home screen, and audio level.

## New

- A new home screen with three 3D objects: Vice (the built-in emulator, on a 1702 monitor), Stream (the C64 Ultimate, on a 1084 monitor) and SID (a SID player, coming soon). Left and right choose; X starts.
- The Stream monitor shows the C64 Ultimate's picture live once PS64 finds it on the network, and TV static until then. The settings icon at the top right chooses another C64 Ultimate or enters its password; the power icon quits.
- Options > Home goes back to the home screen. The emulator pauses there and carries on where it was when you choose Vice again.
- Options > Audio sets the sound level: left and right step from 0 dB down to -30 dB, then Off; Cross turns the sound off and on. The C64 Ultimate stream and the emulator each keep their own level.
- The C64 Ultimate stream and the emulator now start at -12 dB: C64 sound was much louder than other PS5 apps.
