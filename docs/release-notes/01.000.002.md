PS64 1.0.2: audio level.

## New

- Options > Audio sets the sound level: left and right step from 0 dB down to -30 dB, then Off; Cross turns the sound off and on. The C64 Ultimate stream and the emulator each keep their own level.
- The C64 Ultimate stream and the emulator now start at -12 dB: C64 sound was much louder than other PS5 apps.
