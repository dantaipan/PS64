PS64 1.0.6: a SuperCPU, an REU and JiffyDOS for the C64, and SID search by musician.

## New

- SuperCPU option (VICE C64): a 20 MHz 65816 with 16 MB of SuperRAM, for software that needs one.
- REU option (VICE C64): a RAM Expansion Unit of 512 KB or 16 MB, with or without the SuperCPU.
- JiffyDOS option (VICE C64): faster disk loading with your own JiffyDOS ROMs, copied into /data/ps64/roms.
- SID search on Assembly64 finds tunes by musician as well as by title.
