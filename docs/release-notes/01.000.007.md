PS64 1.0.7: more control of the C64 Ultimate with its firmware 1.2.1, and a mouse.

## New

- C64 Ultimate firmware 1.2.0 and newer: keys are held down for as long as you hold them, so games that need a held key (SPACE, F-keys, SHIFT) work.
- Ultimate menu control (firmware 1.2.0 and newer): while the menu is open, the D-pad moves through it, Cross selects and Circle goes back.
- Mouse option (C64 Ultimate firmware 1.2.1 and newer, VICE C64): a 1351 mouse on port 1 for GEOS, C64 OS and other mouse software. The right stick moves it, and R1 and L1 are its buttons. The joystick moves to port 2 while the mouse is on.
