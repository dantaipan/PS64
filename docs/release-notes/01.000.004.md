PS64 1.0.4: cartridges such as fast loaders, music on the home screen, and the SID player plays the C64 Ultimate's tunes.

## New

- Cartridge option: put a cartridge such as Epyx FastLoad (your own .crt) in the emulator's port, or set the C64 Ultimate's.
- Home music: a random SID plays on the home screen (Square for another, Triangle to turn it off; its volume, 0 to 10, is in the home screen's settings).
- SID player: with a C64 Ultimate connected, a C64 Ultimate list browses its storage and plays its .sid files (on PS64 or on the Ultimate).

## Other

- Enhancements to the UI.
