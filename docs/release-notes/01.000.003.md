PS64 1.0.3: a SID player.

## New

- SID player: play C64 music from the SID folder (/data/ps64/sids) or from Assembly64, on the built-in reSIDfp emulation or on a C64 Ultimate's SID chips. Subtunes, playlist with shuffle and repeat, song lengths from HVSC, and a visualiser.

## Other

- Enhancements to the UI.
